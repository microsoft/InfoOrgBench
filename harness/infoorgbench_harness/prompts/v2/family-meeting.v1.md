The records are notes about meetings and, when provided, the full transcripts of those meetings. Each project is a series of meetings held by one team. Organize the records by project, then by meeting.
- Use exactly the meeting folders listed under "This collection" and no others. Within a project, meeting numbers are in chronological order.
- A transcript is the record of one meeting. Place it in its listed folder.
- Place each note in the folder of the meeting whose own discussion contains what the note states: the decision, action, question or discussion it summarizes. A meeting that only recaps or mentions content from another meeting does not contain that content.
- Judge each note by its content. Different projects can cover similar subjects, and the meetings of one project can return to the same topics.
