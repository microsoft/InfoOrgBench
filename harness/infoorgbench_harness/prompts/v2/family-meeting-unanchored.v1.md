The records are notes written about several separate meetings. Organize them by meeting, then by note type.
- Create one folder for each distinct meeting represented in the records and, inside it, folders for note types such as overview, action, decision or problem when the content supports those distinctions.
- Put notes describing the same meeting in the same meeting folder even when different meetings discuss similar product topics.
- Base meeting grouping on the combination of discussed decisions, actions, prototype state, roles and chronology expressed by the notes.
- No meeting or note-type folder names are supplied. Choose short descriptive names.
- More than one semantic organization may be reasonable; choose the best-supported hierarchy from the visible notes.
